About me

I'm Mohamed Abdelaziz, a first-year engineering student at Minia University in Egypt, passionate about game development, cybersecurity, and building cool things that actually work. Last year I volunteered as a mentor at Hack Club STEM October, and that experience shaped how I learn and share what I know.

I've shipped multiplayer Unity games, built AI assistants on tiny hardware, written Python automation tools, and explored both sides of cybersecurity. I like going deep on things that most people would just use off the shelf.

When I'm not coding, I'm playing games, watching anime, or overclocking hardware to do things it absolutely shouldn't.
